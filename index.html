PSA × Blood Test Correlation Analyzer for Prostate Cancer Patients

Upload two Excel files — lab results and diagnosis dates — to automatically compute how PSA correlates with blood sugar, lipids, blood cells, liver/kidney function and other blood tests.

🔒 All data is processed only in your browser and is never uploaded to any server.

1Lab results (Excel)

Required: patient ID, specimen, test name, date, result. Non-blood specimens are excluded.

2Diagnosis dates (Excel)

Required: patient ID and first prostate cancer diagnosis date. If there are several sheets, the one with a diagnosis-date column is picked automatically.
